# Otium: choose the visual direction

[Open the ten interactive studies](https://quiver.fisher.sh/static/reports/otium/2026-10-16-analog-directions/). Switch between Brief, Video, Reader and Library, then try the directions on your phone.

Use the Article sample picker to compare typical VTDigger, POLITICO and 404 Media content, plus the long-headline stress case. Actual metadata and images are paired with labelled length-matched teaser paraphrases.

All ten are serif-led and analog in character. Buffer is the reference for polish and interaction. The upper-right page fold appears in every prototype. These are independent studies, not changes to the live app.

```ask
type: multi
id: shortlist
label: Which two or three directions should we take further?
help: Use Notes for the typography, navigation, spacing or media treatment you want to combine.
notes: true
other: true
options:
  - 01 Paperlight | Otium feels like a small stack of carefully chosen reading cards, with the composure of a native app and the warmth of paper held in your hand.
  - 02 A little room | An intimate literary reading room gives each story a centered serif title, a generous photographic pause, and one warm invitation to read.
  - 03 Listening room | Each story is a carefully framed object in a quiet room, given space to hold your attention before you choose to enter it.
  - 04 Measure | A contemporary reading instrument that pairs expressive serif headlines with the precision and breathing room of Swiss typesetting.
  - 05 Afterglow | Otium becomes a quiet evening instrument, with warm serif typography suspended above a dark, recessed picture frame.
  - 06 Softbound | A small, softly bound volume for a finite sitting, with friendly Fraunces type and confident native controls.
  - 07 Porcelain | A cool ceramic reading sheet rests inside a frosted personal library, keeping the story still and the rest of the app outside its edges.
  - 08 Clay | A reading room shaped by warm clay, rounded serif lettering, and the unhurried pleasure of a small independent bookshop.
  - 09 Folio | A contemporary publisher's reading app, with crisp white space, lyrical serif type and the physical clarity of a single open page.
  - 10 The reading room | A deep forest reading room makes each story feel like an unhurried sitting, with sturdy ivory serif typography and quiet controls at its edges.
```

The chosen direction will retain Otium's existing functionality. These previews simulate account actions locally and use real article metadata with adapted teaser text and an illustrative video. Browser checks cover Chromium and WebKit at phone sizes; a physical iPhone and e-ink review is still needed for the selected implementation.
