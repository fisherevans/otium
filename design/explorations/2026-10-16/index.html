The first three fixtures are near their source's median headline and teaser lengths. The fourth is VTDigger's longest headline, with a 4:3 composite image. The same stories, bylines, dates and source images appear in every style. Each style retains its image framing, so the comparison exposes cropping as well as type and spacing.

What is exact: publisher headlines (dash typography normalized), authors, dates, image assets, source links and measured lengths. What is adapted: teasers are original paraphrases within 2% of the measured feed-description length. VTDigger's feed includes a repeated title and link boilerplate; that space is retained in the fixture rather than quietly shortened. Readers show a labelled excerpt preview and a link to the publisher, not copied full articles.

Otium stores feed descriptions after stripping tags and limiting them to 500 bytes. None of these measured descriptions reached that limit. The prototypes then clamp the visible teaser and, when necessary, reduce image height and headline size to keep one brief on screen. This is a prototype fitting rule, not the production solver; selected production work must be measured again with that solver.

The VTDigger cohort came from two RSS pages, including opinion, obituaries and podcast posts as well as news. A third page was checked to establish the date boundary. The Barre story is syndicated from Vermont Public. POLITICO uses its politics feed only; its article page blocked automated retrieval, so its metadata and canonical link come from its public RSS. 404 Media combines 15 RSS entries and five public listing excerpts for the older dates. Source images are measured at the URLs supplied by those endpoints, not claimed to be original camera resolution. One POLITICO item had no feed image; the four selected fixtures all have images.
