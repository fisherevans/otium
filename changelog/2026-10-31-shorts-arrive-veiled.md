# Shorts arrive veiled; no Next in full screen; a one-time sound hint

Fisher noticed the next clip scrolled in at full brightness, then dimmed once it
landed, then brightened again moments later as it loaded fast - a flicker rather
than a transition. Incoming Shorts cards now wear the veil already, and when the
player takes the clip over its veil starts at that same depth and lifts across the
expected start time, dissolving into the video as it plays.

The floating Next in full screen added little (you swipe) and sat over captions on
tall phones; it is gone. Articles in a full-screen session are padded only for the
safe area.

Sound starts off, so the first muted Short of a session now shows a small
tooltip pointing at the sound control - "Press this to turn the sound on." -
once per session in the tab; it leaves after a few seconds, on a tap, or when
sound comes on.
