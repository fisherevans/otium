# Ten analog design studies

**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Type:** decision
**Author:** codex

## What changed

Added ten independently conceived, interactive visual directions under `design/explorations/2026-10-16`, with a comparison gallery and review form. The fold in the prototype logo is at the upper right. Production app code and deployment are unchanged.

## Why

The first iPhone UX pass improved the shared flows but did not meet Fisher's aesthetic bar. He requested ten distinct mockups before choosing an overhaul. Otium should feel analog and serif-led, while borrowing Buffer's native interaction polish. Each study preserves the one-brief vertical swipe and calm inset media as the central experience.

## Real-content extension

Replaced the fictional article comparison with four publisher fixtures: near-median VTDigger, POLITICO and 404 Media stories, plus a 149-character headline stress case. Measured 79 feed/listing entries over October 10-16, retaining exact title, author, date, image and length metadata. Teasers are labelled paraphrases within 2% of the source length. Added an article picker, source links, sampling limits, image credits and per-item numeric evidence. This reveals wrapping and cropping that the tidy fictional examples hid. Production code remains unchanged.

After seeing the real-content layouts, Fisher asked for smaller, easier-to-read titles. Brief headlines now start at 28px (26px narrow) with looser leading, and descriptions retain a 16-17px reading size rather than yielding their legibility to the headline.
