# Trimmed full-screen chrome, a darker loading state, a building screen

Fisher's review of full-screen Shorts: too much floating chrome, and a loading
state that should look stranger. Full screen now drops the bottom pill: the creator
strip (time, sound, CC, full screen, ···) is the bottom element and its ··· opens
the session menu, where "Post details" replaces the card's separate details entry;
Next is a light outline floating bottom-right. The header shrinks to the wordmark
and ···, with Library and Aa folded into that menu. The clip sits between the
header band and the strip, so YouTube's in-embed title and caption rows no longer
collide with ours. Full screen now applies only to sessions that contain Shorts;
a News session keeps its normal layout.

While a clip loads or buffers it sinks into shadow and saturated colour drifts and
morphs through it (conic swirl plus morphing blobs), rather than the earlier soft
glow over a fully lit poster.

Starting a session from Home used to grey the buttons (the untouched default
looked selected) and hang for ~5s. The tap now switches straight to a small
sliding-block "Building your session" screen that carries through the route change
until the first card. The wait itself was mostly one query: see
2026-10-31-session-build-pool-query.md (#187), ~3x faster.
